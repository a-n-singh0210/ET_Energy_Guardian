# EnergyGuardian AI — 60-second elevator pitch

> ~160 words · natural speaking pace. Pause at the em-dashes.

India imports 88% of its oil — and nearly half of it sails through a single
chokepoint: the Strait of Hormuz. When that's threatened, refiners have hours to
react and a nine-and-a-half-day reserve. Today that response is manual and slow —
McKinsey found economies without a response-intelligence layer take 47 days
longer to recover.

EnergyGuardian **is** that layer. It reads live news and market signals, scores
disruption risk by corridor and supplier in real time, then — in one click —
simulates the shock: the Brent spike, the import gap, the hit to GDP — and
generates an executable procurement reroute and reserve-drawdown plan. Signal to
recommendation in seconds, not weeks.

What sets us apart: it's **live**, it's **transparent** — every number traces to
a documented assumption, no black box — and it genuinely uses **all six**
suggested technologies, from a multi-agent orchestrator to a supply knowledge
graph. Free to run, and ready today.
